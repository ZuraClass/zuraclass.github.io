1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Zuralogic Ecosystem, we collect only the information necessary to provide our services:

  • Account & Profile Data: Name, email address, company name, avatar, and portfolio details when you register via /auth/ or access the Creator Hub.
  • Marketplace & Billing Data: Purchase records, invoice details, and Creator payout coordinates processed securely through our payment gateway partners.
  • Usage & Support Logs: Queries submitted to our ZuraAI Assistant (/gethelp/) and interaction metrics used to improve our libraries and templates.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to:

  • Authenticate your access to ZuraClass courses, ZuraAppMarket downloads, and the Creator Hub (Dashboard, Portfolio, Profile).
  • Process marketplace transactions and disburse Creator revenue.
  • Deliver technical support, security updates, and enterprise consultation requests.

3. Google Workspace & AppSheet Data Privacy

Many applications and templates on Zuralogic are designed to run on your own Google Workspace, Google Spreadsheets, AppSheet, or Google Apps Script environment. Once you copy or deploy a template into your own cloud account, your operational business data resides entirely in your own Google Drive / Cloud database—Zuralogic does not have access to your internal company spreadsheets or records.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Infrastructure

We never sell your personal data to third parties. We only utilize trusted enterprise infrastructure providers—such as GitHub Pages for static repository hosting, payment processors for checkout, and cloud authentication services—strictly to operate the Zuralogic platform.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to view, update, export, or request permanent deletion of your account data at any time through your Creator/Member Profile settings or by contacting our support team.

6. Contact Our Privacy Team

For any privacy-related inquiries or GDPR/data deletion requests, please contact us via Get Help or email privacy@zuralogic.com.
